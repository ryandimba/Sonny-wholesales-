Welcome to sonny Sonny-whalosale, your go-to destination for high-quality, refillable perfumes. We are passionate about providing sustainable and affordable fragrance solutions to our customers in Kenya.

Our mission is to reduce waste and promote eco-friendly practices by offering refillable perfume options. We believe that everyone deserves to enjoy their favorite scents without harming the environment.

We source our perfume oils from reputable suppliers, ensuring that our products are safe and of the highest quality. Our friendly staff is always ready to assist you in finding the perfect fragrance to suit your needs.
